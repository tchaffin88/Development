Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: March 2026

DeckFuel accepts returns for sealed Pokémon TCG products within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unopened, in original factory-sealed condition, and free of damage or alteration.

Non‑returnable items

  • Opened products
  • Singles, code cards, or loose packs
  • Clearance items
  • Digital goods

Return process

  • Contact us at info@deckfuel.co with your order number.
  • We will provide return authorization and instructions.
  • Refunds are issued after inspection of the returned item.

Return shipping

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to an error on our part.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will resolve the issue promptly.
